Legal Insights

Common Legal Questions

General information on frequently raised legal topics. It is not legal advice and may not apply to your situation.

Laws and procedures change. Please seek independent legal advice for your specific matter.

Under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, a cheque must be presented within its validity period. After the bank returns it unpaid, a written demand notice generally has to be sent to the drawer within 30 days of receiving the return memo.

The drawer then has 15 days from receiving the notice to make the payment. If payment is not made, a complaint can generally be filed within one month after that 15-day period ends. Missing any of these steps can affect the case, so the dates should be checked early.

Bail is sought by a person who has already been arrested or is in custody, so that they may be released on conditions. Anticipatory bail is sought by a person who fears arrest, before the arrest takes place.

Anticipatory bail is dealt with under Section 482 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, which replaced Section 438 of the Code of Criminal Procedure. Courts decide each application on its own facts.

A legal notice is a formal written communication sent through an advocate. It sets out the facts, the grievance and what is being demanded.

It is commonly used before recovery proceedings and in cheque dishonour, contractual and property disputes. In some matters a notice is a legal requirement; in others it gives the other side a chance to respond before a case is filed.

Identity proof, and any notices, agreements, receipts or correspondence connected with the matter. If proceedings have already started, bring the court orders or case numbers. In a cheque matter, bring the original cheque and the bank's return memo.

A short written timeline of events is also very helpful.

Many claims are subject to limitation periods under the Limitation Act, 1963, and different matters have different periods. Delay can also make documents and evidence harder to gather, so it is sensible to take independent legal advice at an early stage.
